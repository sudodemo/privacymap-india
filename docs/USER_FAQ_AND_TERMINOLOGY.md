# PrivacyMap India — User FAQ & Terminology

## Purpose

This guide explains PrivacyMap India in plain language for users who are not privacy, legal or technology specialists.

PrivacyMap is an assessment and readiness tool. It helps an organisation understand how it handles personal data, identify areas that may need attention, plan improvements, record governance decisions and collect evidence of remediation.

PrivacyMap is not a certification service and completing an assessment does not by itself establish legal compliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is PrivacyMap India?

PrivacyMap India is a browser-based privacy assessment tool designed to help organisations understand their personal-data processing and prepare for the Digital Personal Data Protection framework in India.

### Who should use it?

Business owners, managers, privacy teams, HR, IT, security teams, compliance teams, process owners and other people who understand how the organisation handles personal data can contribute. You do not need to be a privacy lawyer or software developer.

### What information should I enter?

Answer based on how your organisation actually works. Use the best available evidence rather than guessing. If something is unknown, record it as unknown where the assessment allows it and follow the resulting recommendation.

### Does PrivacyMap send my assessment answers to PrivacyMap servers?

The assessment is designed to process assessment responses locally in the browser. Users should still review the application's Privacy Assurance information and their own browser/device environment.

### What is the DPDP readiness score?

The score is an assessment indicator generated from the answers and mapped controls. It is intended to show relative readiness and areas needing attention. It is not a government score, legal certification, audit opinion or guarantee of compliance.

### What score should we try to achieve?

There is no legally prescribed PrivacyMap score that proves compliance. The practical goal is to address identified gaps, reduce material privacy risk, establish appropriate governance and evidence, and move the assessment toward a consistently strong readiness position. A high score with incomplete or inaccurate answers should not be treated as proof of compliance.

### Why can a finding be high risk?

Risk reflects the combination of the potential impact of a privacy issue and the likelihood or exposure indicated by the assessment answers. A high risk does not automatically mean the organisation has violated the law; it means the issue deserves stronger attention or review.

### What are Key Privacy Findings?

Key Privacy Findings are the principal issues identified by the assessment. Other report sections may refer back to the same findings. Clicking a finding title should take the user back to its original entry in Key Privacy Findings.

### What is a treatment plan?

A treatment plan records what the organisation intends to do about an identified risk, including ownership, status and supporting actions.

### What is residual risk?

Residual risk is the risk that remains after planned or existing controls and treatments are considered. It is not the same as saying that all risk has been eliminated.

### Why is management approval sometimes required?

Some residual-risk decisions may need an accountable authority to review and approve them. Completing a risk decision and obtaining its formal approval are separate governance activities.

### What is evidence and closure?

Evidence and Closure records proof that an action or control was addressed and verified. Examples may include policy references, procedure documents, screenshots, tickets, training records, configuration records or other appropriate organisational evidence.

### Why do I need to provide an owner and review date?

Privacy work needs accountability and follow-up. An owner identifies who is responsible for the action or decision, while a review date establishes when it should be reconsidered.

### What are the assessment steps?

The assessment moves from organisational context and data processing through findings, treatment, residual-risk decisions, DPDP mapping, governance, remediation tracking and evidence/closure. Some later steps become available only after the information required for the preceding step is complete.

### What is Industry and Business Type used for?

Industry and business type provide context so PrivacyMap can present relevant business processes and improve the usefulness of its intelligence. They do not automatically determine that an organisation is compliant or non-compliant.

### What are Data Entry / Collection Channels?

They describe where personal data enters or is collected by the organisation, such as web forms, mobile apps, email, telephone, messaging apps, social media, physical forms, APIs and AI assistants.

### Why is AI prompts / AI assistants a collection channel?

People can enter personal data into AI tools. PrivacyMap therefore treats AI prompts / AI assistants as a first-class data-entry channel so organisations can consider what information is entered, why it is entered, who provides the service, retention/training settings where known, and other relevant processing facts.

Using an AI assistant does not automatically create a privacy finding.

### Why are WhatsApp and Telegram grouped together?

Messaging products change over time. PrivacyMap therefore uses a general Messaging Apps category and can optionally record the specific provider/platform. This avoids creating a separate assessment field for every messaging product.

### Can I resume later?

Yes. Assessment continuity supports local saving and resume. Export/import and restore capabilities are also available where provided by the application.

### Can someone edit an exported JSON file?

A JSON file is data, not a tamper-proof signature. Someone with access to the file can potentially edit it. PrivacyMap validates imported structure and values to reduce unsafe or malformed input, but import validation cannot prove that the contents are truthful or originally produced by an authorised person. Organisations should use appropriate internal controls for assessment packages.

### Does PrivacyMap provide legal advice?

No. PrivacyMap provides assessment guidance and readiness information. Where an important legal interpretation or organisation-specific decision is required, obtain appropriate professional advice.

## Assessment score and status language

- **Readiness score:** an indicator of how well the recorded answers align with the application's readiness model.
- **Risk score / risk level:** an indicator used to prioritise privacy issues.
- **Finding:** an identified privacy issue or condition requiring attention, review or treatment.
- **Pass:** the relevant check or condition met the application's defined criterion.
- **Review / Attention:** information indicates that the user should inspect the issue or provide additional evidence.
- **Fail:** the defined condition was not met.
- **Complete:** the required information for that workflow stage has been provided.
- **Pending:** an action or decision still requires completion or approval.

## Important user principle

PrivacyMap rewards accurate understanding and improvement, not simply a higher number. Do not select a favourable answer merely to increase the score. The value of the assessment comes from identifying the real state of personal-data processing and acting on the gaps.
