# V1.4 Validation Design

## Validation is not certification

The reviewer validates the organisation's assessment and remediation state. PrivacyMap does not independently verify evidence or certify statutory compliance.

## Lifecycle

DRAFT → ASSESSED → REVIEWED → VALIDATED → RE-ASSESSMENT

## Evidence coverage

A field counts as covered when it has at least one evidence reference. Future versions can score evidence quality separately.

## Legal mapping

Do not hard-code legal conclusions into risk rules. Keep legal mapping as a versioned data layer with effective dates because the DPDP Act and Rules have staggered commencement provisions.

## Executive report

The executive report should answer five questions:
1. What personal data do we collect?
2. Where does it enter and flow?
3. What are our biggest risks?
4. What evidence and remediation exist?
5. What remains open and when should we review again?
